# Post-fix window sweep on local NVMe at 96k, try-lock on, one factor at a time. On Aug 13 the
# 16xlarge NVMe pair preferred the 17,000 us fsync window over metal's 1,000 (466,640 vs 463,718
# at 60k; 511k vs 481k at 100k+). A short fsync window means many more batches and many more
# parks, which pre-fix meant many more membarriers even on NVMe. If the optimum moves toward
# short windows with the try-lock, that is the same bug showing up on the fast tier.
#
#   CELLS_FILE=cells/nvme-trylock-windows.txt REPS=2 BENCH_DURATION=15 SWEEP_TLS_MODE=strict bash scripts/run-cell-sweep.sh
